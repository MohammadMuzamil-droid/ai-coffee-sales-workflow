# Public-copy privacy review

Builder's own QA, not a third-party audit. Scope includes Markdown/JSON, eight JPEGs, final PDF text/pages/metadata and filenames.

Additional solid raster masks/crops were applied to retained public baseline copies. Masks cover unnecessary name fragments, exact matching timestamp and corporate address/tax text. A nonessential profane word is partly masked for presentation; numeric correction is unchanged. No originals or baseline files were edited. No AI image regeneration or replacement historical numbers were used.

[Measured changes](redaction_changes.json) use baseline-public-image pixel coordinates, not original master-canvas coordinates. Crops remove blank margins/unrelated partial cards/UI edges; catalog captions disclose context limits. Existing masks sometimes hide ordinary words; complete verbatim conversation retention is not claimed.

Customer identifiers, addresses/locality/postcodes, phone/account/reference/tracking/barcode values were reviewed. Candidate professional name/contact is intentional. Supplier names and the sender alias on the shipping label are masked (text, images and PDF), after a later privacy review. No original screenshots or private readbacks are included in this public package.

Amounts/quantities can still be recognized by someone who knows a transaction. Redaction reduces identifiers but does not guarantee absolute anonymity or legal compliance. No zero-PII claim is made. Final QA records final-pixel checks and actual metadata; manifest supplies measured sizes/hashes.
